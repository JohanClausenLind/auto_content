## 2026-09-13 (later still) — the audio for that minute, and a master that was quietly illegal

**Changed**
- Four defects in `audio/sfx.py`, all affecting every clip. Windows were butt-joined with `-c copy`
  and are now overlapped with an **equal-power** crossfade (sqrt ramps: uncorrelated noise summing
  to 1.0 in amplitude sums to 0.7 in power, dipping the bed 3 dB at every join); each window used
  `seed + i`, so one shot sounded spliced; levels are matched to the median window; and
  `flatten_envelope` divides out each window's own slow shape, capped at 9 dB.
- `library_trim_db` reads `loudness.integrated_lufs` per sound, because `footsteps_grass_loop`
  records `gain_limited_by: 'true_peak'` and sits 16 LU below every other bed: sparse transients
  have a high crest factor, so a caller assuming one target places the sound inaudibly.
  `audio.mix.master` is wired in: true-peak limiter, two-pass EBU R128 in `linear=true`, a trim when
  the linear pass overshoots, aiming `encode_headroom_db` low because AAC raises the peak again.
- `speak_continuous` speaks the whole script as one paragraph and cuts it at the pauses the model
  left; `match_line_levels` brings each cut toward the median, capped at 3 dB so intended shading
  survives. Now the default in `audio.continuity` and `stage_synthesize_narration`, with a
  `continuous_take` widget and `narration.continuous_take` to restore the old path.
- The cache key is one hash over the **whole script**, because a cut depends on where its
  neighbours' words fell; a word-count mismatch raises `ContinuityError` rather than falling back
  silently to beat-at-a-time, which is the behaviour the change exists to remove.
- The cut is found in the audio, not the word times: the quietest 20 ms window within 400 ms, taken
  at the middle of the quiet region because windows tie in digital silence and `argmin` parks the
  cut against the preceding word, with an 8 ms taper; a test pins it as aligner-independent.

**Measured**

  own decay made a sawtooth, 55.5 dB and nine ramps -> 36.8 dB, LRA 10.8 -> 3.0.
