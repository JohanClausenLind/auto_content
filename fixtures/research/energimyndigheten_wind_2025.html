Wind power supplied 21 percent of Sweden's electricity in 2025

Preliminary statistics show that wind power generated 34.9 TWh in 2025, about 21 percent of Sweden's total electricity generation of 166 TWh. The share has roughly doubled since 2018, when wind supplied 11 percent.

The growth was driven by new turbine installations, improved siting, and lower financing costs, the agency writes in its annual report.
